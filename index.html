About Me

Hi, I'm Meera! I'm a budding web developer who enjoys creating simple, clean, and modern websites that are easy to use and visually appealing.

I started my journey into web development because I love the idea of turning an idea into something people can actually see and interact with. I'm currently building my skills in HTML and CSS while exploring the world of frontend development.

What I do: I design and build responsive websites — sites that work well on phones, tablets and desktops — for small businesses, restaurants, fashion brands, schools, and personal brands. I especially enjoy designs that are simple but still make a strong impression.

I'm always learning, creating, and looking for new ideas to bring to life on the web. ✨
